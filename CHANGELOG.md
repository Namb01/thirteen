# Changelog

## 2026-11-19 - Patch 1.02

- Added a shuffle sound when the cards are dealt

## 2026-11-18 — Sound update

- Sound effects for playing cards and bombs, with a few versions of each picked at random
- Mute button in the top corner (remembered in your browser)
- Bomb animation: a flash, shockwaves and sparks, plus a screen shake when a four of a kind or consecutive pairs hits the table
- The 1st place winner leads the next game in the same room; the 3♠ rule only applies to the first game

## 2026-11-18 — First release

- Tiến Lên (Thirteen) for 4 players: play against the computer, or online with friends using a 4-letter room code
- The room host can fill empty seats with computer players; the game starts once every seat is filled
- Play continues until everyone has a place (1st to 4th)
- Being dealt all four 2s is an instant win
- Shuffle, deal and play animations
- Card art by Mesmedir (CC0), based on Dmitry Fomin's deck
- Players who leave mid-game are replaced by a computer; you can rejoin after reloading the page
